Quick picks for beginners

Start with one goal and a realistic budget – you don’t need 10 different products.

Goal: build muscle Budget: student-friendly Training age: 0–2 years

Creatine Monohydrate

#1 priority

Safest, most researched supplement for strength & size. Go for a simple, unflavoured creatine monohydrate with no extra nonsense.

Dose: 3–5 g per day • Timing: whenever • Loading phase: optional

From ~8–12 € / month

Whey / Protein Powder

Food first – but a simple whey or plant protein helps you hit your protein target on busy days, or when appetite isn’t great.

Use when: you can’t reach your daily protein goal from regular food.

From ~15–25 € / month

Basic Health Stack

Vitamin D, omega-3, and magnesium can be worth it if your diet and sunlight exposure are lacking. Think of these as health insurance, not magic muscle pills.

Only if bloodwork / diet suggests you need it • Budget: flexible / optional

From ~10–20 € / month
