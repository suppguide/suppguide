What is SuppGuide?

Stop wasting money on random supplements.
Beginner Guide

SuppGuide is a tiny, no-BS guide that shows you which supplements actually make a difference when you train – and which ones are just expensive sugar.

We start with three essentials for most lifters:

✓ Whey or plant protein ✓ Creatine monohydrate (3-5 g / day) ✓ Basic vitamin D & omega-3 (if needed) ✕ “Fat burners” & detox teas ✕ Overpriced “test boosters”

You’ll see simple recommendations, sample stacks and honest notes about when you probably don’t need a supplement at all.

Pick your main goal

Build muscle & size

Focus on calories, protein and sleep first. A basic whey + creatine stack is enough for 99% of people.

Lose fat & stay strong

Protein powder can help you hit daily targets. Caffeine is often more useful than fancy “burners”.

More energy & focus

Start with proper hydration and consistent sleep. Pre-workout should be optional, not a crutch.

How SuppGuide works

  1. Choose your goal – bulk, cut or performance.
