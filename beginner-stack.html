Beginner supplement guide

New to fitness and supplements? This page shows you what you actually need — and what you should avoid wasting money on.

First rule: supplements are not magic

  • Supplements help only if your diet and training are correct.
  • No supplement can replace food, sleep, or consistency.
  • More products ≠ better results.

Supplements beginners can consider

  • Protein powder – only if you can’t reach daily protein from food.
  • Creatine – well-studied, safe, and effective for strength.
  • Vitamin D – useful if you don’t get enough sun.

Supplements beginners should avoid

  • Fat burners promising fast results
  • “Testosterone boosters” with no real evidence
  • Unnecessary stacks with many unknown ingredients
