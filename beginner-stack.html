Quick picks for beginners

Start with these – you don’t need 10 supplements to get results.

#1 priority

Creatine Monohydrate

Most researched supplement for strength and performance. Look for a simple, unflavoured creatine monohydrate – no crazy blends needed.

Typical use: 3–5 g per day · Cost: very cheap per month

support

Protein Powder

Convenience tool – basically food in powder form. Helps you hit your daily protein target on busy days when cooking isn’t realistic.

Typical use: 20–40 g on days you’re low on protein.

optional

Basic Health Stack

Simple additions like vitamin D, omega-3, and magnesium can support overall health – but they only matter after sleep, food, and training are in a good place.

Best for: people with flexible budget who want a clean baseline.
