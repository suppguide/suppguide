The Beginner Supplement Stack

If you’re new to lifting or fitness, you don’t need 10 supplements. This is the minimal, proven stack that actually works.

STEP 1 · PRIORITY

Creatine Monohydrate

Creatine is the most researched supplement for strength and performance. Safe, cheap, and effective for beginners.

  • ✅ Improves strength & performance
  • ✅ No timing needed
  • ✅ 3–5g per day

Skip fancy blends. Plain creatine monohydrate works.

STEP 2 · SUPPORT

Protein Powder

Protein powder is food, not magic. Use it only if you struggle to reach daily protein from meals.

  • 🥩 Food first
  • ✅ Whey or plant-based both work
  • ✅ 1–2 scoops as needed
STEP 3 · OPTIONAL

Basic Health Stack

Only add this if your diet or bloodwork shows you need it.

  • Vitamin D
  • Omega-3
  • Magnesium

What beginners should skip

  • ❌ “Mass gainers”
  • ❌ Testosterone boosters
  • ❌ Fat burners
  • ❌ Mega stacks
