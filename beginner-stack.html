Recommended beginner stack

These 3 supplements are usually enough for most beginners when diet and sleep are on point.

Creatine Monohydrate

#1 priority • strength & power

  • Most researched supplement for strength & performance.
  • 5 g per day, every day, timing doesn’t really matter.
  • Look for plain creatine monohydrate with no extra fillers.
Daily dose: 3–5 g

Whey / Protein Powder

Support • daily protein target

  • Helps you hit your protein goal when food alone isn’t enough.
  • Use on busy days – not a magic muscle builder by itself.
  • Pick a flavor you enjoy and that sits well with your stomach.
Use: 1–2 scoops on busy days

Basic Health Stack

Optional • overall health

  • Usually includes vitamin D, omega-3, and magnesium.
  • Comes after sleep, food, and training are consistent.
  • Helps close small gaps in your daily nutrition.
Use: Only if bloodwork / diet suggests it

Beginner FAQs

Do I need all three supplements?

No. Creatine alone is already a strong start. Protein powder is just a food shortcut, and the health stack is optional.

How long should I use this stack?

Think in months, not days. Focus on training, sleep, and consistent nutrition first.

Is this safe for beginners?

For healthy adults, these are some of the most researched and commonly used supplements. If you have a medical condition, always talk to your doctor first.
