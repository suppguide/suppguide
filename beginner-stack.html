Before you buy anything

3 quick checks you should always do

Every supplement on your shelf should pass these basic checks. If a product fails one of them, you probably don’t need it.

  • ✓
    Is the basic nutrition okay?
    Enough protein, sleep and water will always matter more than any powder.
  • ✓
    Can you explain what it does in one sentence?
    If you can’t explain it, you shouldn’t pay for it.
  • ✓
    Is the dose realistic?
    Many “pro” blends hide tiny doses in big labels. Dose matters more than the design.

Safe starting point

Your first stack (for most people)

For healthy adults, these three are usually enough for a long time:

  • 1
    Protein powder – only if you can’t hit your protein from food.
  • 2
    Creatine monohydrate – cheap, studied, no drama.
  • 3
    Caffeine – from coffee or a simple pre–workout, not 12 ingredients.

Have any medical condition or medication? Talk to a doctor first. A website can’t replace that.

The “big three” explained

Protein, creatine & pre–workout in 20 seconds each

Here is what each one actually does, how to use it and when you can completely skip it.

Protein powder
Just a convenient food, not magic
Goal: muscle gain / fat loss Start: 1 scoop / day

Use it when: your daily protein from chicken, eggs, yogurt, etc. is not enough. Skip it when: you already hit your protein target with normal food.

Creatine monohydrate
The most boring but effective choice
Goal: strength & power Dose: 3–5 g per day

Same dose every day, timing is not super important. Drink enough water. No fancy “alkaline / liquid / nano” versions needed.

Pre–workout
Energy boost (but watch your sleep)
Goal: focus & pump Key: caffeine amount

Check the label for caffeine per serving. Avoid huge doses at night if you care about recovery and sleep.

How to start without messing it up
Follow these steps in order. Don’t add 5 new supplements in one week.
1
Fix your basics first
Sleep, training plan and food quality will decide 80% of your results. Supplements are the last 20%.
2
Add one product at a time
Start with the simplest: protein or creatine. Track how you feel for 2–3 weeks before adding anything else.
3
Keep a tiny log
Write down: what you take, dose, time, and how your training and sleep feel. Super short notes are enough.
4
Review once a month
If you don’t feel or see any benefit, don’t be scared to remove a product. A smaller, smarter stack saves money.
Quick FAQ
Questions most beginners ask
Short, honest answers — no extreme promises, no “take this and gain 10 kg in a month” nonsense.
Is creatine dangerous? ?
For healthy people with normal kidneys and liver, standard doses of creatine monohydrate are considered safe. If you have any medical issue, ask a doctor before you start.
Do I need a “mass gainer”? ?
Usually no. Most gainers are just carbs + protein. You can build the same thing with rice, oats, milk and a normal whey.
How many supplements is “too many”? ?
If you can’t explain what each product does and why you take it, you’re already using too many. Most people are fine with 1–3 items.
