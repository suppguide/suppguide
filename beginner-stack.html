Simple, safe supplement stack for beginners.

This page is for someone completely new. We keep the stack minimal, focus on training and sleep first, and only add a few proven products.

Plan summary
1. Fundamentals first: food & sleep

Before supplements, make sure your calories, protein, and sleep are under control. Think of supplements as an extra 5–10% boost on top of the basics.

2. Starter stack (minimum)

The idea is to keep it simple and affordable. A solid beginner stack usually looks like:

  • Whey protein – to help you reach your daily protein target.
  • Creatine monohydrate – 3–5 g every day, same time.
  • Basic multivitamin – optional, to cover small gaps.
3. When to use them?

Use them consistently, not randomly. Creatine can be taken any time of day. Protein is most useful after training or between meals, and multivitamin works best with breakfast.

Medical note

This is not a medical prescription. Always consider your own health conditions and talk to a doctor if you’re unsure about anything.

Quick guide
Training & protein

Train 3–4 days per week with a full body or push/pull/legs plan. Aim for about 1.6–2.2 g of protein per kg of bodyweight daily.

How to take the stack
  • Creatine: 3–5 g once per day (no loading phase needed).
  • Whey: 1 scoop after training or when you’re low on protein.
  • Multivitamin: 1 serving with breakfast.
What to expect

With consistent training, sleep, and this stack, you should notice better recovery, strength progress, and lean muscle gain over the first few months.
