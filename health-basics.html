HEALTH BASICS

Supplements that actually make sense

Evidence-based health supplements that can support training, recovery and long-term health – without hype or marketing tricks.

These are not magic. They simply cover common deficiencies and make consistent training easier and safer over the long term.

What matters with health supplements

  • Real benefits shown in human research
  • Fixing common deficiencies first, not chasing “boosters”
  • Safe long-term use at reasonable doses
  • Good quality products from trusted brands

Key supplements and what they do

Omega-3

Omega-3 fatty acids (EPA & DHA) support heart, brain and inflammation health – especially if you rarely eat fatty fish.

  • Supports cardiovascular and brain health
  • May help manage joint pain and soreness
  • Most useful when dietary fish intake is low

Vitamin D

Vitamin D is important for immunity, hormones and bone health. Many lifters who live indoors or in northern climates are low.

  • Supports immune function and mood
  • Helps maintain healthy bones and teeth
  • Blood test is ideal; avoid mega-dosing blindly

Magnesium

Magnesium is involved in muscle contraction, sleep quality and stress management. Many diets don’t provide enough.

  • Can improve sleep quality and relaxation
  • Supports muscle and nerve function
  • Best taken in the evening with a light snack

How to choose and use these supplements

Smart dosing

  • Omega-3: 1–3 g combined EPA + DHA per day with a meal
  • Vitamin D: follow blood test & doctor guidance
  • Magnesium: 200–400 mg elemental in the evening

Product quality

  • Pick brands that share 3rd-party testing or certificates
  • Skip “proprietary blends” and under-dosed formulas
  • Avoid miracle claims or aggressive marketing

Who can probably skip them

  • People who already eat fatty fish several times per week
  • Those with plenty of safe sun exposure & good diet
  • Anyone following medical advice that says “no”
