What creatine actually does

Strength & power

Creatine increases phosphocreatine stores, helping you push more reps and heavier loads during training.

Muscle fullness

Muscles store more water intracellularly, leading to a fuller appearance without being fat gain.

Not magic

Creatine doesn’t work without proper training and nutrition. It enhances, not replaces, effort.

Creatine types we recommend

Creatine monohydrate

  • Most researched & proven
  • Best price/performance
  • 3–5 g daily

Micronized creatine

  • Better mixability
  • Same effectiveness
  • Easier digestion for some

Expensive blends

  • Often unnecessary
  • No proven advantage
  • Mostly marketing
