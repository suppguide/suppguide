What creatine does — and what it doesn’t

Improves strength & power

Creatine increases phosphocreatine stores in muscles, helping you perform more reps or lift slightly heavier.

Supports muscle gain indirectly

By improving training performance, creatine can support muscle growth over time.

Is NOT a steroid

Creatine is naturally found in food and in your body. It does not affect hormones.

How to use creatine properly

Daily dose

  • 3–5g per day
  • No need to cycle
  • Consistency matters most

Timing

  • Any time of day works
  • Post-workout is convenient
  • Not time-sensitive

Best form

  • Creatine monohydrate
  • No need for fancy blends
  • Purity > marketing
