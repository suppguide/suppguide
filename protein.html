What protein does — and what it doesn’t

Supports muscle growth

Protein provides essential amino acids needed to repair and build muscle when combined with resistance training.

Helps hit daily intake

Shakes are a convenience tool, not magic. They help reach daily protein targets when food intake falls short.

Does NOT replace food

No powder beats whole foods. Quality protein supplements should complement, not replace, a proper diet.
