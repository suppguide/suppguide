HOW WE RATE PROTEIN

Protein quality

Protein per serving, amino acid profile, and source (whey isolate, concentrate, blend).

Ingredients

Added sugar, gums, artificial colors & unnecessary fillers.

Digestibility

Mixability, stomach comfort and lactose friendliness.

Price / performance

Cost per 25–30 g of protein and overall value.

Our protein short-list

Exact brands will depend on your country, but these categories show what we look for.

Best overall whey

High-protein whey isolate

  • ≈ 24–27 g protein per 30 g serving
  • Low fat & carbs, minimal lactose
  • Short ingredient list, no useless blends

Ideal for most lifters who want maximum protein with minimal calories.

Budget pick

Whey concentrate

  • Good protein content at lower price
  • Slightly more carbs & fat than isolate
  • Best if you tolerate lactose well

Lactose-free / vegan

Blended plant protein

  • Mix of pea, rice or soy for full amino profile
  • Check protein per serving (aim ≥ 22 g)
  • Watch out for added sugar and flavors

How to use protein powder

Protein powder is just a convenient food. It doesn’t replace real meals – it helps you reach your daily protein target.

  • Aim for ~1.6–2.2 g protein per kg of bodyweight per day.
  • Use 1–2 shakes daily to fill gaps you can’t get from food.
  • Mix with water or milk after training or whenever it’s convenient.
  • Focus first on total daily protein, not “anabolic window”.
