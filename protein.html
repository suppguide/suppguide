What protein does — and what it doesn’t

Supports muscle growth

Protein provides essential amino acids needed to repair and build muscle when combined with resistance training.

Helps hit daily intake

Shakes are a convenience tool — not magic. Useful when food protein intake falls short.

Does NOT replace food

No powder beats whole foods. Protein supplements should complement a proper diet.

How to use protein properly

Daily intake

  • 1.6–2.2 g per kg bodyweight
  • Split across meals
  • Consistency matters most

Timing

  • Any time of day works
  • Post-workout is convenient
  • Not time-sensitive

Best type

  • Whey concentrate or isolate
  • Check protein per serving
  • Avoid sugar-heavy blends
